Guide to reading the data
Smart Money Analysis — Zenith
SM VAL — Smart Money
Total large funds flowing into a stock from institutional players. Larger = higher accumulation potential.
BM VAL — Bad Money
Total large funds flowing out of a stock. Bad Money dominance = distribution / large-scale selling.
CM — Clean Money
SM Val − BM Val. Large positive = inflow dominates. Negative = distribution dominates.
RSM — Smart Money Ratio
SM / (SM + BM) × 100%. Above 70% = strong accumulation signal.
Clean Money Formula
CM = SM − BM
Positive → accumulation dominant
Negative → distribution dominant
RSM > 70% → strong SM signal
